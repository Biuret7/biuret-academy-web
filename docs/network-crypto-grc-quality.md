Public coverage/blueprint files expose outcomes and mappings only.

Worked and assessed cases use different services, populations, subnets, time offsets or boundary conditions to require transfer. Existing score thresholds, cooldowns, attempt limits and passing records are retained. New GRC attempts require course 19; already-passed paths remain passed.
