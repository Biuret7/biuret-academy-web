## Learning experience

- `free-studio.html` offers six bilingual teaching units: analytical scope, networks, Linux permissions, cryptography, identity/session/access, and investigation/reporting.
- Each unit has explanations, a worked example, an independent task, personal notes, a formative check with explanation, and related free course/practice links.
- A beginner route distinguishes deeper study from the existing nine-lesson credential requirements.
- Three synthetic practice desks have two evidence variants each: URL parsing, traditional permission bits, and sign-in log triage. Extraction checks explicitly do not grade report quality.
- A traditional permission calculator supports independent reasoning. ACLs and path traversal are explicitly outside its model.
- Recall review is due immediately after an incorrect answer, or in 1/3/7 days after a correct answer according to stated confidence. Dates are suggestions, not proof of mastery. Previous answers are not preselected on review.
- Learners choose a suggested 10/20/30-minute session, without a countdown.
- A six-field practice portfolio has a self-review rubric and JSON/TXT downloads. Entered notes are retained; no account identifiers or email are added automatically.
- Notes and formative state stay in this browser under the signed-in account's key. The notebook is not cloud synchronized and its local checks cannot mint verified XP, coins or credentials.

The same route is linked from the sidebar, homepage, catalogs, progress, review, profile, membership and Foundations path. First-time learners enter the studio from the homepage; returning learners retain their verified next-step shortcut.
